Common questions

Does Thread sync between devices?

No. Thread intentionally stores data only on the current device. Use Settings → Data & archive → Export if you want to move a copy yourself.

How do I restore an archive?

Open Settings → Data & archive → Restore archive, then choose a ZIP previously exported by Thread. Restore merges missing or archived records without overwriting active records.

Can I remove old data?

Yes. Delete one moment or check-in from its editor, clear the selected day beside that date, or use Data & archive for a date range or all local data. Clear operations create a recoverable local archive first.

Why is a photo still in Photos after I remove it from Thread?

If “Save captured photos” was enabled, Thread also added the photo to the system photo library. Remove that separate copy in the Photos app.

Which devices are supported?

The iPhone app supports iOS 15 or later. The Home Screen widget requires iOS 17 or later.

When you write

Include the Thread version shown in Settings, your iOS version, and a short description of what you expected and what happened. Do not send private notes, photos, or exported archives unless they are essential to the issue.

常见问题

Thread 会在设备之间同步吗？

不会。Thread 有意只把数据保存在当前设备上。如果想自行转移副本，请使用“设置 → 数据与归档 → 导出”。

如何恢复归档？

打开“设置 → 数据与归档 → 恢复归档”，选择此前由 Thread 导出的 ZIP。恢复只会合并缺失或已归档的记录，不会覆盖仍在使用的数据。

可以清理旧数据吗？

可以。在编辑器中删除单个时刻或打卡，在日期旁清理当天，或在“数据与归档”中选择日期范围或全部本地数据。清理前会先建立可恢复的本地归档。

为什么从 Thread 删除照片后，“照片”里还有一份？

如果开启了“保存拍摄的照片”，Thread 会同时把照片加入系统照片图库。那一份需要在“照片”App 中另行删除。

支持哪些设备？

iPhone App 支持 iOS 15 及以上版本；主屏幕小组件需要 iOS 17 及以上版本。
