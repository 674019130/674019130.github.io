Privacy, without fine print.

Thread is designed so the developer does not receive your moments, notes, photos, check-ins, or usage activity.

What Thread stores

Thread stores the moments, labels, colors, notes, photos, events, check-ins, and preferences that you create. This information remains in the app’s local storage or its local widget container on your device.

What Thread collects

Nothing. Thread has no developer-operated server, account system, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking technology. The developer does not receive personal data from the app.

The website remembers only your English or Chinese display choice in local browser storage. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Photos and permissions

Camera and photo-library access is requested only when you choose to attach or capture a photo. A selected photo is copied into Thread’s local storage. If you enable “Save captured photos,” a camera photo is also added to your system photo library. Thread does not transmit these photos.

Export, sharing, and deletion

Exports happen only after you request them. iOS then lets you choose where the local archive goes. Any destination you select is governed by that destination’s own privacy terms.

You can remove individual moments and check-ins, clear a day or date range, or clear all local data from within Thread. Thread first creates a recoverable local archive. Removing the app removes its sandboxed data; photos separately saved to the system photo library remain there until you delete them in Photos.

Changes and contact

If Thread’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy declaration will be updated before the relevant version is released.

隐私，不藏在小字里。

Thread 的设计原则是：开发者不会收到你的时刻、备注、照片、打卡或使用活动。

Thread 保存什么

Thread 会保存你创建的时刻、标签、颜色、备注、照片、事件、打卡和偏好设置。这些信息只留在设备上的 App 本地存储或本地小组件容器中。

Thread 收集什么

不收集任何数据。Thread 没有开发者运营的服务器、账户系统、分析、广告、崩溃上报或追踪技术。开发者不会从 App 收到个人数据。

本网站只在浏览器本地记住你选择的中文或英文，不使用分析、广告或追踪 Cookie。

照片与权限

只有当你主动添加或拍摄照片时，Thread 才会请求相机或照片权限。所选照片会复制到 Thread 的本地存储。开启“保存拍摄的照片”后，相机照片也会写入系统照片图库。Thread 不会传输这些照片。

导出、分享与清理

只有你主动发起时才会导出。随后由 iOS 让你选择本地归档的去向；你所选择的目标服务适用其自身隐私条款。

你可以在 Thread 内删除单个时刻或打卡，清理某一天、一段日期或全部本地数据。Thread 会先建立可恢复的本地归档。卸载 App 会移除沙盒数据；已经另存到系统照片图库的照片仍需在“照片”中自行删除。

变更与联系

如果 Thread 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会在相关版本发布前更新本政策和 App Store 隐私声明。
