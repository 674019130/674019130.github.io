Implementation notes / 实现技巧

The official VoiceBeam renders the default, pill and mobile presets. A simulated level drives all three, while processing switches to the component’s own sweep. Reduced motion pauses the effect.

官方 VoiceBeam 渲染默认输入框、录音胶囊和手机三种预设。模拟音量驱动光晕，处理中切换为组件自带的扫光。减少动态效果时暂停动效。
