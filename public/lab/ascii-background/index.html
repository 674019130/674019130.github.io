How the atmosphere is made↗

先把风景照片柔焦，再将明暗映射到蓝紫、朱红色阶。每一个等宽字符取自它所在位置的亮度：字符的密度随风景变化，轮廓因此连贯。

字符和底图在同一个平面上。可选的「漂移」只让整个平面缓慢移动，不随机刷新字符，所以没有闪烁。原参考是静态图，这段移动是实验室自己的延伸。

这是用不同照片重新实现的视觉研究，并非原图或作者源码。查看实现 ↗

First, soften a landscape photograph and map its light and shadow to lavender, blue, and vermilion. Each monospaced character samples the brightness beneath it, so the texture follows the contours of the landscape.

The characters and photograph share one plane. Optional Drift moves that plane slowly without randomly refreshing the characters. The reference images are static; this movement is an extension made for the lab.
